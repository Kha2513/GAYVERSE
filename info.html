About Buildverse

What is Buildverse?

Buildverse is an online virtual sandbox platform dedicated to user-generated 3D building games. Build, share your creations, and connect with other builders in an interactive web environment.

Features

Create your own unique 3D models and places using building tools, customize your blocky 3D avatar with custom colors, publish your .bdvr files directly to your personal profile page, and explore what others have built!

Community Guidelines

Be respectful to fellow players and builders. Keep builds, profiles, and discussions appropriate, creative, and fun for everyone sharing the platform experience.
